## ✨ Features

- **Main Features:**
  - The sequence of pockets is arranged correctly (The same order as in the real game).
  - Configurable bet amount.
  - You can decide on what pocket you want to bet. The program supports to bet on colors (red, black, green) and (multiple) numbers at once.
  - The win will be determined correctly at the end of a spin. It is calculated precisely to gain the "House" money in the long term.
  - While running the program you can look at your balance and the possible prices.

## 👨‍🎓 Explanations

A short explantion on every german term.

- "Wie hoch ist dein Einsatz?" = "What amount do you want to bet?"
- "Du besitzt nur ..." = "You only own ..."
- "Worauf wettest du? (g,r,s,zahl)" = "On what pocket(s) do you want to bet?" "g" = green, "r" = "red", "s" = "black", "zahl" = "number"
- "Guthaben" = "balance"
- "Gewinn" = "win"

There are a few more terms I won't explain because they're mostly irrelevant and occure rarely.
